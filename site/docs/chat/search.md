# Search

The search box at the top of every page finds pages, plays, settings and your own work. If you don't find what you need, the last row starts a new chat with what you typed.

**Where to find it:** the search box in the page header, labelled "Search Clara…"

## Open search and find something

1. Click the search box, or press **Ctrl K** (Cmd K on a Mac) or **/**.
2. Before you type, you see **Recent** (the results you opened last) and **Suggested** pages.
3. Type a few letters. For example, "Acme Analytics".
4. The first result is already highlighted. Press Enter to open it, or click the result you want. Use the arrow keys to highlight a different one.
5. Press **Esc** to close search without opening anything.

<video src="/assets/docs/chat/search-open-product.mp4" poster="/assets/docs/chat/search-open-product.jpg" autoplay muted loop playsinline aria-label="Pressing Ctrl K, typing the product name Acme Analytics and pressing Enter on the first result to open the product page"></video>

The status line under the box says "Searching", "No results", "1 result" or a number of results.

## What search covers

Results are grouped in this order: **Pages**, **Plays**, **Settings**, **Products**, **Campaigns**, **My work**, **Agents**, **Workflows**, **Skills** and **Chats**.

- Pages, plays and settings are searched as you type.
- Your own items, such as products, work, agents and chats, load after you type two characters.
- If some of them can't be searched, you see "Some of your work couldn't be searched just now. Try again in a moment."
- **Chats** includes the chats you own and the chats shared with you.
- You only see what you are allowed to open.
- **Recent** is kept in your browser, for each organization.

## Ask Clara

The last row is always **Ask Clara**. After you type, it reads **Ask Clara —** followed by your text, with "Start a chat with this" on the right.

1. Type a question, for example "What should I post about our new dashboard?".
2. Pick the **Ask Clara** row by clicking it. If it is already highlighted, press Enter. Use the arrow keys to move to it otherwise.

<video src="/assets/docs/chat/search-ask-clara.mp4" poster="/assets/docs/chat/search-ask-clara.jpg" autoplay muted loop playsinline aria-label="Typing a question into search, selecting the Ask Clara row and a new chat opens with the question already sent"></video>

**You should see:** a new chat that has your text already sent.

> **Note:** Search finds things in Clara. It doesn't search the text of your knowledge sources. To search those, use **Find information** in **Brand & knowledge**. See [Give Clara your context](/docs/context/brand).

## FAQ

**Why can't I find a teammate's chat?** Search only lists chats you own and chats shared with you. Ask your teammate to share it. See [Chat history and sharing](/docs/chat/chats).

**How do I search my knowledge instead of my work?** Use **Find information** in **Brand & knowledge**.

**What is the shortcut?** **Ctrl K** on Windows and Linux, Cmd K on a Mac, or **/** when you are not typing in a field. **Esc** closes search.
