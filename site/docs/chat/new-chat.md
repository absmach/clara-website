# Home and New chat

Home is where you start a conversation with Clara. Describe an idea, ask a question or ask for a draft, and Clara replies in the chat. Drafts open in a preview panel beside the conversation.

**Where to find it:** sidebar → **New chat** (the first item in the sidebar)

## What Home shows

- A greeting and the message box. The message box says "Describe an idea, ask a question, or make something new…".
- Three quick cards under the message box: **Write something** ("Turn an idea into a first draft"), **Plan a launch** ("Bring a bigger story together") and **Find a new angle** ("Explore your market and ideas"). Each card opens a play, usually in a short setup dialog where you write a brief and click **Run now**. The link **Explore all plays** opens the full list.
- **Needs your attention.** Drafts waiting for your review, drafts that failed, and pitches waiting for approval. Each item has **Open the draft** or **Review the pitch**. If you are allowed to review it, you can also click **Approve** or **Reject** right here, or **Review claims** when the draft has claims to check. When nothing is waiting, it says "You're up to date. Nothing needs your attention."
- **Pick up where you left off.** Your recent work, with a **View all** link to **My work**. When there is nothing yet, it says "Nothing generated yet. Ask Clara above to get started."

## Start a chat

1. Click **New chat** in the sidebar.
2. Click the message box and type your request. For example: "Write a short LinkedIn post announcing a new dashboard in Acme Analytics."
3. Press Enter, or click the send button (the up arrow). Press Shift+Enter for a new line.
4. Watch the answer appear in the chat. Click **Stop** while Clara is replying if you want to cancel.

The chat gets a title from your first message. You can rename it later. See [Chat history and sharing](/docs/chat/chats).

## Control what Clara uses

The row under the message box has these controls:

- **+** ("Add to this chat") opens a menu with **Upload from computer**, **Add a link** and, inside an existing chat, **Make a video**.
- **Product** sets which product the chat is about. **Organization** (the default) means the chat isn't about one product. Pick a product so its voice and sources apply.
- **Web** lets Clara search the web and read the pages it cites. Click it to switch to **Web off**, and Clara answers from your knowledge and files only.
- **Add skills** lets you pick a skill for this chat.
- The microphone button dictates your message when your browser supports it.

On Home, a panel under the message box shows **Brand guidelines** with the text "Organization default · Applied automatically", or "Product overrides apply where set · Applied automatically" when you pick a product. Clara always uses your brand guidelines. Click **View guidelines** to read them.

## Attach a file or a link

1. Click **+**, then **Upload from computer**, and choose a file. Documents, images, audio and video are accepted.
2. A chip with the file name appears above the message box. Click the **x** on the chip to remove it.
3. To add a web page, click **+**, then **Add a link**, paste the address into the field that says "https://..." and click **Add**. Click **Cancel** to close the field.
4. Type your message and send it. Wait for uploads to finish first.

<video src="/assets/docs/chat/new-chat-attach-file.mp4" poster="/assets/docs/chat/new-chat-attach-file.jpg" autoplay muted loop playsinline aria-label="Clicking + then Upload from computer, choosing a PDF of release notes, typing a request and sending it; the chat opens with the message and the file, shows Reading your files while Clara reads it, then Clara replies and starts a draft"></video>

**You should see:** the chat opens right away with your message and the file names, and the line "Reading your files…" while Clara reads them. The message box is locked until she's done. Long documents can take a little while. Then Clara replies.

> **Note:** If you leave the chat while Clara is still reading your files, the message isn't sent. Clara tells you so and puts your text back in the message box when it's empty. Attach the files again and send it once more.

> **Note:** You can attach up to 6 files at a time, and up to 5 links per message. Documents can be up to 10 MB. Images, audio and video have their own limits, and the message box tells you the limits when a file is too large. If a file type isn't supported, you see "That file type isn't supported. Try a document, PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF, audio or video file."

## Start a play from chat

You don't need a setup dialog. Describe what you want, for example "Write a LinkedIn post about our new release", and Clara works out the play, the product and the details from your message and your attachments. If it needs something, it asks in the chat.

When Clara has what it needs, one of two things happens:

- It starts straight away. Clara says "Started" followed by the play name and "I'll show the draft here when it is ready."
- It waits for you. You see "Waiting for your go-ahead:" followed by the play name, with **Go ahead** and **Cancel**.

To continue, click **Go ahead**. Typing a short "yes" does the same. Click **Cancel** to drop the request.

The **Draft preview** panel opens beside the conversation. It shows "Preparing your draft…" and then "Draft ready", with **Open editor**, **Download** and a copy button. On a narrow screen, switch between **Conversation** and **Draft preview** at the top.

> **Note:** Clara waits for your go-ahead when you didn't ask for the work, or when your autonomy setting asks for approval first. See [Choose your level of autonomy](/docs/automate/autonomy). A request that waits is dropped after half an hour.

## Refine a draft

While a draft is open, the message box says "Ask Clara to refine this draft…". Describe the change, such as "Shorten it to three sentences", and Clara revises the draft. In the preview panel you can also click **Open editor**, **Download** or **Copy draft**.

## The chat header

At the top of a chat you see the title, **Copy conversation** and **Share conversation**, the product chip, and a link that says who can see the chat: **Only you**, **Shared with** a number of people, or **Product members**. See [Chat history and sharing](/docs/chat/chats).

> **Note:** Drafts made in a private chat stay private to you until you share the chat or tag it with a product. If you open a chat that was shared with you as a viewer, you see "You have view-only access to this chat." and no message box. Clara answers one message at a time: if you send another too soon, you see "A reply is already in progress. Wait for it to finish." If Clara takes too long, you see "Clara took too long to reply. Try again." Click **Try again** under "That reply didn't finish."

## FAQ

**How do I make Clara use only my own knowledge?** Click **Web** so it reads **Web off**. Clara then answers from your knowledge and files only.

**Why can't I attach this file?** The message box shows the reason under the file chip: the type isn't supported, the file is too large, or you reached 6 files.

**How do I start a specific play from chat?** Name it in your request, for example "Write an X thread about this release". Clara starts it, or asks for your go-ahead.

**Where did my draft go?** In the **Draft preview** panel of the chat, and in **My work**.
