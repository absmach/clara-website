# Chat history and sharing

Every conversation with Clara is saved as a chat. You can find recent chats in the sidebar, see all of them on one page, and share a chat with people in your organization.

**Where to find it:** sidebar → **Recent chats**, and **View all chats**

## Find your chats

- The sidebar lists your **Recent chats**. A chat that Clara is still answering shows a working indicator.
- Click the small archive icon next to the heading to switch to **Archived chats**. Click it again ("Show recent chats") to go back.
- Click **View all chats** to open the **All chats** page. It has **Recent** and **Archived** filters and a **New chat** button.
- When a list is empty, you see "No chats yet" ("Start a chat and it will be listed here.") or "No archived chats" ("Chats you archive are kept here.").

## Rename a chat

1. In the sidebar, point at the chat and click the three dots ("Actions for" followed by the chat title).
2. Click **Rename**.
3. Type the new name in the **Chat title** field and click **Save**.

**You should see:** the new title in the sidebar.

## Archive, restore and delete

1. Open the three-dot menu of a chat in the sidebar.
2. Click **Archive** to move the chat to the archive. In the archived list the same menu shows **Unarchive** instead.
3. To remove a chat for good, click **Delete** and confirm "Delete this chat? This can't be undone."

On the **All chats** page, each row has **Archive** (or **Restore** in the **Archived** filter) and a trash icon to delete.

To bring back an archived chat:

1. Click **View all chats**.
2. Click **Archived**.
3. Click **Restore** on the chat.

## Share a chat

1. Open the chat and click **Share conversation** (the people icon at the top right). You can also click the visibility link under the title, such as **Only you**.
2. The **Share this conversation** dialog lists the owner (marked **Owner**), everyone you gave access to, and the product if the chat is tagged with one (marked **Product**).
3. Click **Add a person**.
4. Under **Person**, choose a member of your organization. For example, Jane Doe.
5. Under **Role**, choose **Viewer (can read)** or **Manager (can continue)**.
6. Click **Grant access**.

<video src="/assets/docs/chat/chats-share.mp4" poster="/assets/docs/chat/chats-share.jpg" autoplay muted loop playsinline aria-label="Clicking Share conversation, Add a person, choosing Jane Doe as a Viewer and clicking Grant access so she appears in the list"></video>

**You should see:** Jane Doe in the list as "Can read" with the role **Viewer** and a **Remove** button. The visibility link now reads "Shared with 1 person".

You can't change a role in place. To change a person's role, click **Remove** next to their name, then add them again with the other role. To stop sharing with someone, click **Remove**. Click **Done** to close the dialog.

## What sharing means

- Sharing a chat also shares the drafts made in it.
- If you tag the chat with a product (the **Product** menu in the message box), everyone with access to that product can open it. Product viewers can read, and product managers can continue.
- Sharing doesn't change who is a member of your organization.
- The chat shows a **Shared** badge in the sidebar and on the **All chats** page. Point at it to see who shared it.
- People you share with get a notification. See [Notifications](/docs/chat/notifications).

> **Note:** Only the owner of a chat can rename, archive, delete or share it, and change its **Web** setting. People you shared it with can't. If you don't have permission to share a chat, you see "You do not have permission to share this chat." Viewers see "You have view-only access to this chat." and can read but not reply.

## FAQ

**Who can see my chat by default?** Only you. The visibility link says **Only you** until you share the chat or tag it with a product.

**If I share a chat, do people see its drafts?** Yes. They can open the drafts made in that chat.

**How do I get a chat back after archiving?** Open **View all chats**, click **Archived**, then click **Restore**.

**Can I delete a shared chat?** Only the owner can. Deleting a chat can't be undone.
