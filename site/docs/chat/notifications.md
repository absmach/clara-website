# Notifications

Clara tells you when a draft is ready, when something failed and when something needs you. Notifications show in the bell at the top of the page, and on the **Notifications** page.

**Where to find it:** the bell in the page header, then **View all notifications**

## Use the bell

1. Look at the bell in the header. A number on it shows how many notifications you haven't read.
2. Click the bell. A list of recent notifications opens, with "N unread" under the **Notifications** heading.
3. Click a notification to open what it is about. It is marked as read.
4. To clear the unread count in one go, click **Mark all as read**.
5. To remove a single notification, click the x on it ("Dismiss notification"). To remove all of them, click **Clear all**.
6. Click **View all notifications** to open the full page.

When there is nothing to show, the bell says "You're all caught up."

## Use the Notifications page

The page says "What Clara finished, what failed and what needs you".

1. Use **All**, **Unread** and **Read** to filter the list.
2. Click **Mark read** or **Mark unread** on a notification.
3. Click the trash icon ("Delete notification") to remove one.
4. Click **Mark all as read** to read everything at once.
5. Click **Delete all** to remove the notifications in the current filter. A dialog called **Delete notifications?** tells you how many, and asks you to confirm with **Delete** or **Cancel**.

When a list is empty, you see "No notifications yet.", "No unread notifications." or "No read notifications."

## Choose which notifications to show

1. On the **Notifications** page, scroll to **Which notifications to show**.
2. Tick the kinds you want to see, and untick the ones you don't: **Content generation**, **Campaign generation**, **Competitive positioning**, **Knowledge syncs**, **Workflow updates**, **Organization invitations** and **Shared chats**.
3. Click **Save preferences**.

<video src="/assets/docs/chat/notifications-preferences.mp4" poster="/assets/docs/chat/notifications-preferences.jpg" autoplay muted loop playsinline aria-label="On the Notifications page, filtering by Unread, clicking Mark read on one notification, unticking Knowledge syncs under Which notifications to show and clicking Save preferences"></video>

**You should see:** "Notification preferences saved."

> **Note:** Muting a kind hides its notifications in the bell and on the page. Clara still creates them, so ticking the kind again shows them again. **Organization invitations** is always shown and can't be turned off.

## FAQ

**Do I get email notifications?** Notifications appear in Clara, in the bell and on the **Notifications** page.

**How do I stop seeing sync notifications?** Untick **Knowledge syncs** under **Which notifications to show** and click **Save preferences**.

**Why can't I turn off invitation notifications?** An invitation that someone sent you shouldn't go unseen, so **Organization invitations** always stays on.
