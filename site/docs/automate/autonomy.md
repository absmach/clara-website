# Choose your level of autonomy

Autonomy sets how much Clara may do without asking you first. You choose a preset for the workspace and then keep approval where it matters.

**Where to find it:** **Settings → Autonomy**

## Choose a preset

Open **Settings → Autonomy** to choose a starting point for your workspace.

- **Assistant:** suggests work. Nothing runs on its own.
- **Co-pilot:** does internal work automatically and asks before external or irreversible actions, such as publishing.
- **Autopilot:** makes actions automatic, including publishing, subject to your settings, guardrails, connected services and the creator's permissions.

Autopilot can keep configured marketing workflows moving without a new prompt or approval at every step. It still needs active workflows and the connections their jobs require.

## Keep approval where it matters

Set an action, channel or product to **Needs approval** to keep a human decision. For example, allow routine social posts to go out automatically while keeping one product's posts in review.

> **Note:** Agent restrictions can be stricter than workspace settings. New agents, including the team created by **Set up your marketing team**, can keep approval requirements under Autopilot. Check each agent's effective settings before you expect it to publish on its own.

## Set the boundaries

Use guardrails for posting frequency, spacing, quiet hours, and topics or competitors to avoid. Guardrails can hold automatic work for approval. The creator's permissions and connected account access still apply, and claim checks can also block automatic publishing.

These controls let you delegate a regular marketing rhythm while keeping decisions that need your judgment in review.

## Pause when plans change

1. Choose **Pause autopilot** to hold automatic actions for approval.
2. To stop future automatic job starts, also pause the agent or the job.

Automatic calendar items wait until you resume and they pass the required checks. Pausing doesn't undo work that has already happened, and existing workflow runs may continue.

## Check overrides after changing presets

Applying a preset resets action, channel and product overrides. Guardrails, pause and agent restrictions stay. Review the confirmation and restore any exceptions you want to keep before you rely on the new preset.

## FAQ

**Does Clara publish by itself?** Only when your effective autonomy settings allow it. Assistant and Co-pilot ask before anything external.
