# Product page and product access

Each product has a page where you check what Clara understands, set a product voice, add sources and see recent work. This is also where you decide who can open and manage the product.

**Where to find it:** sidebar → **Products** → a product

## Tour of the page

The header shows the product name and "Product context · " followed by your organization. If you manage the product, you also see **Edit details** and **Manage access**. The page has three tabs: **Overview**, **Sources** and **Activity**.

**Overview** has four parts:

- **What Clara understands.** A summary of the product with a badge: **Reviewed context**, **Draft context**, **Generating**, **Needs another look** or **Not reviewed**. Click **Read the full understanding** (or **Open understanding** if there is no summary yet) to open "How Clara understands" followed by the product name.
- **Brand voice.** Shows **Inherited** (the organization's guidelines) or **Customized**. **View shared guidelines** opens the organization's [Brand guidelines](/docs/context/brand).
- **Marketing direction.** Three answers: "Why choose this product?", "Current offer" and "Business goal". Three guided steps sit below them: **1. Add evidence**, **2. Review understanding** and **3. Review your plan**.
- **Made with this context.** Recent drafts for this product, with **View work** to see them all.

## Let Clara learn the product

1. Open the **Sources** tab and click **Add source**. Add the product's website, docs, a GitHub repository or a file. See [Knowledge sources](/docs/context/knowledge-sources).
2. When the sources are ready, go back to **Overview** and click **Read the full understanding** (or **Open understanding**).
3. In the dialog, open **Review and edit** and click **Generate from evidence**. Clara writes the understanding from the product's sources.
4. Read it. The dialog shows **Capabilities**, **Who it helps**, **What we should not claim**, **Limitations**, **What sets it apart** and **Competitors**. To fix something, edit the form under **Review and edit**: **Category**, **Evidence-backed summary**, **Capabilities**, **Limitations**, **Supported use cases**, **Unsupported use cases**, **Differentiators** and **Competitors**, one statement per line. In the form, "Who it helps" is **Supported use cases**, "What we should not claim" is **Unsupported use cases** and "What sets it apart" is **Differentiators**. Then click **Save draft**.
5. When it is accurate, click **Verify profile**. The badge changes to **Reviewed context**.

> **Note:** Clara writes the understanding from the product's own evidence. A draft says "A draft: check it against the sources before relying on it." Review it before you rely on it.

## Set a voice for one product

1. On **Overview**, find **Brand voice** and click **Customize for** followed by the product name.
2. In "Brand guidelines for" the product, fill in **Brand voice**, **Audience** and **Writing guidelines**. Open **More voice details** for **Style**, **Keywords** and **Avoid list**.
3. Under **Logo, colours and fonts**, add a logo address or upload a PNG, JPEG or SVG file up to 1 MB. Set **Primary colour**, **Accent colour**, **Background colour**, **Text colour**, **Secondary colour**, **Heading font** and **Body font**. Leave a field blank to use the organization's value.
4. Click **Save customization**.

<video src="/assets/docs/context/product-customize-voice.mp4" poster="/assets/docs/context/product-customize-voice.jpg" autoplay muted loop playsinline aria-label="Clicking Customize for Acme Analytics, typing a product brand voice and audience and clicking Save customization; the Brand voice card shows the new voice and the badge Customized"></video>

**You should see:** the badge **Customized**. To go back, open **Customize** again and click **Reset to shared guidelines**.

The product's name, website, description, **Tagline**, **LinkedIn** and **X / Twitter** live under **Edit details** ("Details for" the product, saved with **Save settings**).

## Set the marketing direction

1. Click **Edit direction**.
2. Answer "Why should your audience choose this product?", "What are you offering now?" and "Which business outcome should the team work toward?"
3. Click **Save direction**.

Your answers guide the next plan. They don't change existing plans, approvals or verified product facts.

## Give people access

1. Click **Manage access**. The dialog is called "People with product access".
2. Under **Person**, choose a member of your organization, for example Jane Doe.
3. Under **Role**, choose **Viewer** or **Manager**.
4. Click **Grant access**.

<video src="/assets/docs/context/product-manage-access.mp4" poster="/assets/docs/context/product-manage-access.jpg" autoplay muted loop playsinline aria-label="Clicking Manage access on the Acme Insights product, choosing Jane Doe and the Viewer role and clicking Grant access; Jane Doe appears in the list as a Viewer, then Done"></video>

- **Viewer** can read the product and what is made with it.
- **Manager** can also edit the product, manage its sources and manage its people.
- Organization admins always have access to every product. They show with the badge **Organization admin** and aren't added by hand.

To change a role, use the selector next to the person (**Viewer** or **Manager**). To take access away, choose **Remove access** and confirm "Remove access?" with **Remove**.

Only organization members can be added. If everyone already has access, you see "Everyone in the organization already has access." Admins can follow **Invite people to the organization**. Other managers see "Ask an organization admin to invite more people."

> **Note:** Viewers can read everything but can't edit: the manager buttons are hidden for them. If you open a product you can't access, you see "That product wasn't found, or you don't have access to it." Chats tagged with a product can be opened by everyone with access to it. See [Chat history and sharing](/docs/chat/chats).

## The Sources and Activity tabs

- **Sources** shows "Sources for" the product, with "Organization-wide context also applies." You can use **Search knowledge**, **Sync all** and **Add source**. People who can't manage sources see **Sync all** and **Add source** greyed out.
- **Activity** shows **Recent work for** the product and **Product conversations**. Click **New chat** to start a chat tagged with this product.

## FAQ

**What is the difference between Viewer and Manager?** A Viewer reads. A Manager also edits the product, manages its sources and decides who has access.

**Do admins need to be added?** No. Organization admins always have access to every product.

**How do I stop a product using the organization's voice?** Choose **Customize for** the product and save your own voice. To go back, choose **Reset to shared guidelines**.

**Why does it say Draft context?** The understanding was generated but nobody has verified it yet. Review it and click **Verify profile**.
