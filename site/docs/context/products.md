# Products

A product is something you sell, such as Acme Analytics. Each product can have its own voice, sources and people. Agents, chats and plays use the product you choose, on top of your organization's shared brand and knowledge.

**Where to find it:** sidebar → **Products**

## See your products

**Products** has the subtitle "The context behind everything you create." Each product is a card with its name, a short description, a source count such as "2 product sources", and a badge: **Custom voice** if the product has its own brand guidelines, or **Organization voice** if it uses the shared ones. Click a card, or **Explore product**, to open it.

You only see products you have access to. Organization admins see every product. When you have none, you see "No products yet", or "You don't have access to any products yet. Ask an organization admin to add you."

## Add a product

Only organization admins can add products.

1. Open **Products**.
2. Click **Add product**.
3. Fill in **Name**, for example "Acme Analytics".
4. Leave **Slug** empty to create it from the name, or type your own. The slug is the product's short address.
5. Add the **Website** and a **Description** that says what the product does, who uses it and what problem it solves.
6. Click **Add product**.

**You should see:** a new card for the product, with the badge **Organization voice**.

> **Note:** Products are private. Nobody except organization admins can see a new product until they are given access. See [Product page and product access](/docs/context/product-page).

## Use a product

- In **New chat**, open the **Product** menu in the message box and pick the product. The default, **Organization** (no product), means the chat isn't about one product. Clara then uses that product's voice and sources.
- In a play, choose the product in the play's dialog.
- Agents and workflows work from your products and sources too.

## Share or delete a product

Each card has a menu ("Product actions") with **Edit**, **Share** and **Delete**.

- **Edit** opens the product.
- **Share** copies the product's link to your clipboard. People still need access to open it.
- **Delete** is for organization admins. It asks "Delete <name>?" and warns "The product and its settings will be removed for everyone. This can't be undone."

## FAQ

**Why don't I see any products?** You only see products you have been given access to. Ask an organization admin to add you.

**What is a slug?** The short address of the product, made of lowercase letters, numbers and dashes. Leave it empty and Clara creates it from the name.

**Do I need a product to use Clara?** No. Without one, Clara uses your organization's brand guidelines and sources.

**Can I change a product's voice later?** Yes. Open the product and choose **Customize**. See [Product page and product access](/docs/context/product-page).
