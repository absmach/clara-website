# Quickstart

This page takes you from a new account to your first approved draft. It takes about ten minutes. Each step ends with what you should see.

**Where to find it:** the sign-up page, then Home

## 1. Sign up

1. Open the sign-up page.
2. Fill in **Full Name**, **Username**, **Email**, **Password** and **Confirm Password**. The username is 3 to 32 characters: lowercase letters, numbers, dashes and underscores. Capital letters are saved as lowercase. The password needs at least 10 characters.
3. Click **Sign up**.

<video src="/assets/docs/start-here/quickstart-sign-up.mp4" poster="/assets/docs/start-here/quickstart-sign-up.jpg" autoplay muted loop playsinline aria-label="Filling in the sign-up form as John Doe and clicking Sign up, then the first chat with Clara appears"></video>

**You should see:** the first-run chat with Clara, with the notice "Welcome! Check your email to verify your account." You can start using Clara before you verify your email.

Already have an account? Open the log-in page, enter your email or username and password, and click **Log in**. When it is shown, **Sign in with Google** also works.

> **Note:** If someone invited you, sign up with the email address they invited. Your invitation appears in the first chat (see step 2).

## 2. Meet Clara

After you sign up, you land in a chat with Clara, not a form. Clara asks short questions, one at a time:

1. **Company name.** This creates your organization (your workspace) and its short web address. If the web address is taken, Clara asks for another one, such as `acme-marketing`.
2. **Timezone.** Click the quick reply that starts with **Use** and shows your browser's timezone, or type a city. The quick reply only appears when your browser shares its timezone.
3. **Industry.** Click one of the quick replies: Software & SaaS, E-commerce, Financial services, Healthcare, Manufacturing, Media & entertainment, Professional services, Education, Real estate & property or Other.
4. **Company size.** Click 1-10, 11-50, 51-200, 201-1000 or 1000+.
5. **Your role.** Click Marketing, Sales, Founder / Executive, Product, Content / Creative or Other.
6. **Website.** Type your address, for example `acme.com`.

Type your answer or click a quick reply. Click **Skip** to skip a question. The company name is the only question you can't skip.

<video src="/assets/docs/start-here/quickstart-onboarding.mp4" poster="/assets/docs/start-here/quickstart-onboarding.jpg" autoplay muted loop playsinline aria-label="Answering Clara's first-run questions for Acme Corp: typing the company name, clicking two quick replies, clicking Skip on the rest, then clicking Take me to Clara"></video>

**You should see:** each answer appear as your message, with Clara's next question below it.

> **Note:** **Skip setup**, at the top right, ends the first-run chat and takes you straight to Home. You skip the recap in step 4. You can fill in the details later.

## 3. Add your website

When you give Clara your website, it adds the site to your knowledge and reads it in the background. You don't have to wait.

**You should see:** Clara says "Added" followed by your address, then "to your knowledge base. I'll read it in the background."

If you click **Skip** instead, nothing is added. You can add sources later in **Brand & knowledge**.

## 4. Check what Clara knows

After the last question, Clara recaps your answers and shows a card called **What Clara knows so far**.

1. Read the recap. It lists the industry, company size, role and website you gave.
2. Look at the card. It shows how many sources Clara has, and lists anything still missing, such as **Company description**, **Audience** or **Brand voice**.
3. Use the links on the card when you want to finish setup now: **Add your first product**, **Edit company details**, **Edit brand and audience** and **Check sources**. You can also continue now and finish later.
4. Click **Take me to Clara**.

**You should see:** Home, with the message box in the middle of the page. The clip in step 2 shows steps 3 and 4.

## 5. Ask for a first draft

1. On Home, click the message box. It says "Describe an idea, ask a question, or make something new…".
2. Describe what you want. For example: "Write a LinkedIn post about Acme Analytics, our app for tracking website traffic. Speak to small marketing teams."
3. Press the send button, or press Enter.

<video src="/assets/docs/start-here/quickstart-first-draft.mp4" poster="/assets/docs/start-here/quickstart-first-draft.jpg" autoplay muted loop playsinline aria-label="Typing a LinkedIn post request on Home and sending it, then the finished draft appears in the preview panel beside the chat"></video>

**You should see:** the chat opens and Clara replies "Started **LinkedIn post**. I'll show the draft here when it is ready." The **Draft preview** panel beside the conversation shows "Preparing your draft…", then "Draft ready" with **Open editor**, **Download** and a copy button.

You can also start from **Plays**: pick a play, fill in the dialog and click **Run now**. See [From a brief to a first draft](/docs/create/plays).

## 6. Review the draft

1. Read the draft in the **Draft preview** panel.
2. To change something, write it in the chat. The message box says "Ask Clara to refine this draft…". For example: "Make the opening shorter."
3. Clara replies, for example "I'll shorten the opening and leave the rest unchanged.", and the preview shows the new version.
4. When you're happy, click **Open editor** in the panel. You can also find the draft later in **My work**.

<video src="/assets/docs/start-here/quickstart-review-draft.mp4" poster="/assets/docs/start-here/quickstart-review-draft.jpg" autoplay muted loop playsinline aria-label="Asking Clara in the chat to make the opening shorter, the Draft preview updates, then clicking Open editor opens the draft's own page"></video>

**You should see:** the draft on its own page, with the status "Draft ready". "Draft ready" means the draft is generated. It is not approved or published.

## 7. Decide what happens next

Nothing is published until you click. What you see on the draft's page depends on the kind of content:

- **Posts Clara can publish for you**, such as a LinkedIn post or an X post: **Publish now** and **Schedule post**. Both need a connected account. You can also use **Edit draft** or **Download**.
- **Content Clara can't publish itself**, such as an article, release notes or an email: **Approve**. Click it to record that your team approved the draft. You then see "Approved by" followed by your name.

> **Note:** **Approve** records that your team approved the draft. It does not publish or schedule anything. For more, see [Review and share your work](/docs/publish/review-and-approvals).

> **Note:** Some actions, such as accepting an invitation, need a verified email address. If Clara says "Verify your email", check your inbox for the link.

## If you were invited

If someone invited you to a workspace, the first chat starts with the invitation instead of the company questions. Click **Accept** to join. You go straight to that workspace's Home, and the company questions are skipped. Click **Decline** to turn it down, or type your own company name to set up a separate workspace.

## FAQ

**Can I skip the first-run chat?** Yes. Click **Skip** on any question after the company name, or **Skip setup** to leave the chat. Clara still needs a company name to create your workspace.

**I was invited. Why don't I see the company questions?** Joining an existing workspace skips them. They are for whoever creates a new workspace.

**What if my organization's web address is taken?** Clara asks for another web address. Use lowercase letters, numbers and dashes, for example `acme-marketing`.

**Where do I change these answers later?** Company details are in **Settings**. If you are not an admin, **Settings** opens **Connections** (tooltip "Personal connections and workspace preferences"). Voice and audience are in **Brand & knowledge** → **Brand guidelines**. Websites and files are under **Sources**.
