# Glossary

Short definitions, in alphabetical order. Where a term has its own page, the term links to it.

**Where to find it:** this page

## Terms

- [**Agent**](/docs/automate/agents). A role you give Clara, with one or more jobs that run on their own. See [Build your marketing team](/docs/automate/agents).
- [**Approval**](/docs/publish/review-and-approvals). The step where a person says yes before a draft is scheduled or published. See [Review and share your work](/docs/publish/review-and-approvals).
- [**Authority tier**](/docs/context/knowledge-sources). How much Clara trusts a knowledge source: **1 · Authoritative**, **2 · Strong evidence**, **3 · Contextual** or **4 · External**. You set it when you add a source, or leave it on **Automatic**.
- [**Autonomy**](/docs/automate/autonomy). How much Clara may do without asking. The presets are **Assistant**, **Co-pilot** and **Autopilot**. See [Choose your level of autonomy](/docs/automate/autonomy).
- [**Brand guidelines**](/docs/context/brand). Your voice, audience, writing rules, logo and colours. Clara applies them to every draft.
- **Campaign.** <!-- TODO link: /docs/create/campaigns --> One brief turned into a set of related drafts.
- [**Claim**](/docs/publish/review-and-approvals). A statement in a draft that Clara checks against your knowledge, such as a feature name or a number.
- **Connection.** <!-- TODO link: /docs/connections/overview --> A link between Clara and another account or tool, such as a social account. Connections appear in the sidebar under **Connectors**.
- **Content piece (draft).** <!-- TODO link: /docs/create/my-work --> One thing Clara has written, such as a post or an article. Drafts are listed in **My work**.
- **Draft ready.** <!-- TODO link: /docs/create/my-work --> The status of a draft that Clara has finished and that is waiting for you.
- [**Knowledge source**](/docs/context/knowledge-sources). A website, file or other material that Clara reads to learn about your company.
- [**Memory**](/docs/context/knowledge-sources). What Clara keeps from its own work, such as conversations, finished drafts and workflow run summaries, so later chats and drafts can find it.
- **Organization.** <!-- TODO link: /docs/account/organizations --> The workspace everything belongs to: people, products, connections and work.
- [**Play**](/docs/create/plays). A ready-made job for one format, such as a LinkedIn post. See [From a brief to a first draft](/docs/create/plays).
- [**Product**](/docs/context/products). Something you sell. A product can have its own voice, sources and people.
- [**Product access**](/docs/context/product-page). What a person can do with a product: **Viewer** can read, **Manager** can also continue the work.
- [**Skill**](/docs/context/skills). A reusable set of instructions that you can attach to a chat or a play.
- [**Studio**](/docs/create/plays). A play that opens its own workspace page instead of a short setup dialog, such as **Competitors**.
- [**Sync**](/docs/context/knowledge-sources). Clara reading a knowledge source again to pick up changes.
- **Workflow.** <!-- TODO link: /docs/automate/workflows --> A step-by-step automation. It runs behind an agent, or on its own.

## Status words in My work

These are the statuses you can filter by in **My work**:

- **Draft ready.** Clara finished the draft.
- **Report.** A research document, such as a positioning analysis, rather than a post.
- **Approved.** A person approved the draft. Approval does not publish it.
- **Needs approval.** The draft is waiting for someone to approve it.
- **Scheduled.** The draft has a publish time.
- **Published.** The draft has gone out.
- **Needs attention.** Something went wrong, or Clara needs you to look.

## Chat visibility words

These appear next to the product chip at the top of a chat:

- **Only you.** Nobody else can open this chat or its drafts.
- **Shared with N.** You gave one or more people access. The label reads "Shared with 1 person" or "Shared with 3 people".
- **Product members.** The chat is tagged with a product, so everyone with access to that product can open it.

See [Chat history and sharing](/docs/chat/chats).
