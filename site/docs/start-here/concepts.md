# Core concepts

These are the building blocks you meet in Clara. Each one has the same name in the sidebar, so you can find it quickly.

**Where to find it:** sidebar, and the organization menu at the top of the sidebar

## The concepts

- **Organization** (workspace). <!-- TODO link: /docs/account/organizations --> The account everything belongs to. Switch between organizations, or add one with **Create organization**, from the organization menu at the top of the sidebar. Members, connections and autonomy are set in **Settings**. If you are not an admin, **Settings** opens **Connections**.
- **Products.** <!-- TODO link: /docs/context/products --> What you sell. Each product can have its own voice, sources and people. Sidebar → **Products**.
- [**Brand & knowledge**](/docs/context/brand). Where Clara learns about you: **Brand guidelines** (voice, audience, writing rules, logo, colours), **Sources** (what Clara reads) and **Skills** (reusable instructions). Sidebar → **Brand & knowledge**. See [Give Clara your context](/docs/context/brand).
- [**Plays**](/docs/create/plays). Ready-made jobs, such as writing a post, planning a launch or finding a new angle. Sidebar → **Plays**. See [From a brief to a first draft](/docs/create/plays).
- **My work.** <!-- TODO link: /docs/create/my-work --> Everything Clara has drafted, with a status on each piece. Sidebar → **My work**.
- **Campaigns.** <!-- TODO link: /docs/create/campaigns --> One brief turned into a set of related drafts. Sidebar → **Campaigns**.
- [**Agents**](/docs/automate/agents). A role you give Clara, with one or more jobs that run on their own. Sidebar → **Agents**. See [Build your marketing team](/docs/automate/agents).
- **Workflows.** <!-- TODO link: /docs/automate/workflows --> The step-by-step automations behind agents, or on their own. Sidebar → **Workflows**.
- [**Autonomy**](/docs/automate/autonomy). How much Clara may do without asking. **Settings** → **Autonomy** offers three presets:
  - **Assistant**: Clara suggests, and nothing runs on its own.
  - **Co-pilot**: Clara does internal work on its own and asks before anything external.
  - **Autopilot**: Clara does everything, including publishing.

  See [Choose your level of autonomy](/docs/automate/autonomy).
- [**Approval**](/docs/publish/review-and-approvals). The step where a person says yes before something is scheduled or published. See [Review and share your work](/docs/publish/review-and-approvals).

> **Note:** **Calendar**, **Advertising** and **Comments** also appear in the sidebar. They are covered in the Publish and engage section of this guide. **Comments** only appears in workspaces that have connected inbox or comment sources.

## FAQ

**What is the difference between an agent and a workflow?** A workflow is a set of steps. An agent is a role that owns one or more workflows and runs them for you.

**Do I need to set everything up before I start?** No. Start with a chat or a play, and add products and brand details as you go.
