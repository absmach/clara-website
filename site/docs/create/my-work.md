# My work

**My work** lists every draft, report and website Clara has made that you can see. Use it to find a draft, check its status and open it. Plays, chats, campaigns and agents all save their results here.

**Where to find it:** sidebar → **My work**

The page says "Good ideas, taking shape." **Create something** takes you to [Plays](/docs/create/plays).

## Find a draft

1. Type in **Search your work** ("Search your work…") to search titles and text.
2. Use **Filter by product** to pick **All products** or one product.
3. Use **Filter by format** to pick **All formats**, a format such as **Blog post**, **LinkedIn post**, **Carousel** or **Newsletter**, or **Documents** for research reports.
4. Use **Filter by status** (see below).
5. Switch between **Cards** and **Table**. The table has the columns Title, Product, Status and Updated.

## Statuses

**Filter by status** offers **All statuses**, **Draft ready**, **Report**, **Approved**, **Needs approval**, **Scheduled**, **Published** and **Needs attention**.

- **Draft ready**: Clara generated the draft. It is not approved or published.
- **Report**: a finished research document, such as a positioning analysis.
- **Approved**: a person approved it.
- **Needs approval**: it is waiting for a person.
- **Scheduled**: it has a publish time, shown on the item.
- **Published**: it went out.
- **Needs attention**: generation failed or the piece is blocked.

While a draft is being made, its badge says **Writing** or **Queued**. These two are not in the filter. A draft that was stopped shows **Cancelled**.

> **Note:** A status filter looks at your newest drafts. If you have a lot of work, the page says "Showing matches among your newest" followed by a number, so very old drafts may not appear under a status filter. Use the search box to find them.

## Download or delete a draft

1. On a card or row, open the menu **Content actions**.
2. Choose **Markdown (.md)**, **Microsoft Word (.docx)** or **PDF (.pdf)** to download the draft. Word and PDF only appear when the service can produce them.
3. Choose **Delete** and confirm "Delete this content? This can't be undone."

<video src="/assets/docs/create/my-work-download.mp4" poster="/assets/docs/create/my-work-download.jpg" autoplay muted loop playsinline aria-label="Opening the Content actions menu on a card in My work and choosing Microsoft Word (.docx)"></video>

Deleting needs the Editor role or an organization admin. It also removes the draft's images and what Clara remembered from it. Website items from **Improve website visibility** have **Website actions** with **Delete**, which removes the website's reports, history and schedule.

## Who can see what

- Drafts of a product are visible to people with access to that product. You never see drafts of products you can't open. See [Product page and product access](/docs/context/product-page).
- Drafts Clara makes in a private chat are visible only to you, to organization admins, and to people the chat is shared with, until the draft itself is shared. See [Chat history and sharing](/docs/chat/chats).
- Drafts made by plays, campaigns and workflows follow the product rule alone.

When nothing has been made, you see "Nothing generated yet" ("Your generated content will show up here once Clara drafts something.") with **Start a play**. When filters match nothing, you see "No matches" ("No content matches these filters. Try widening your search.").

## FAQ

**Why can't I see a teammate's draft?** It may belong to a product you can't open, or it came from a private chat that isn't shared with you.

**What does Draft ready mean?** Clara generated it. It does not mean approved or published.

**How do I get a draft out of Clara?** Open the menu **Content actions** and download it, or open the draft and use **Download**. See [The content page](/docs/create/content-page).
