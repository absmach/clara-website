# Generation details and retry

When a draft fails, you can look at what happened and try again with feedback. Failures are written in plain language, not as raw errors.

**Where to find it:** a failed draft → **View details and retry**, or a campaign piece's menu → **Retry generation**

## Retry a failed draft

1. Open the failed draft, or the piece in its campaign.
2. Click **View details and retry** on the draft, or **Retry generation** in the piece's menu.
3. The page is titled "Content Generation". It shows the **Organization**, the **Product**, when it **Started** and when it **Finished**.
4. Under **Retry generation**, read "Retry this failed generation using the same request. Add feedback to correct it before Clara tries again."
5. Optional: write feedback in **Retry comments**, for example to use a different angle, remove unsupported claims or shorten the draft.
6. Click **Retry generation**.

**You should see:** "Generation retry queued." and "Clara is working on this generation" while it runs. When it finishes, **Open generated content** takes you to the draft.

The section **Agent run** shows "What Clara did to produce this, step by step."

## Other ways to recover

On the failed draft itself you can click **Try again**, **Retry with notes** ("What should be different this time?"), or **Accept anyway** to keep the draft as it is. See [The content page](/docs/create/content-page).

> **Note:** Retrying needs the Editor role or an organization admin.

## FAQ

**Why did generation fail?** The page shows the reason in plain words. If it isn't clear, retry with feedback in **Retry comments**.

**How is Accept anyway different from a retry?** **Accept anyway** keeps the draft you have, with the points the review raised left as claims to review. A retry makes a new one.
