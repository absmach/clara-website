# The content page

Every draft has its own page. This is where you read it, revise it with Clara or by hand, review its claims, approve it and download it. Publishing and scheduling are covered in [Review and share your work](/docs/publish/review-and-approvals).

**Where to find it:** sidebar → **My work** → a draft

## What the page shows

The header has the title, a status badge, the format, the product and "Updated" with the time. The badge is one of **Draft ready**, **Needs approval**, **Scheduled**, **Published**, **Needs attention** or **Report**. A note under the draft says "Draft ready means generated, not approved or published."

Tabs above the text switch the view: **Preview** and **Raw Markdown**. The icon for **Fullscreen preview** opens it larger. Newsletters also show their **Subject** and **Preview text**.

On the side:

- **Post images** (see [Images on a draft](/docs/create/images)).
- **Sources & context**, with **Manage sources**.
- **About this draft** ("About this report" for a report): the **Product**, the **Format** and **Visibility**.
- **Generation details**: token counts and step timings.

## Ask Clara for a change

1. Find **Edit with Clara**. It says "Ask for changes where this draft began, with the whole conversation in view."
2. Click the box ("Describe a change, or paste a reference image…") and describe the change, for example "Make the opening shorter."
3. Optional: click **Attach reference** to add a reference image. It is used to explain your idea and isn't added to the post.
4. Click **Send**. Enter sends, Shift+Enter starts a new line, and the up and down arrows bring back earlier requests.

<!-- CLIP: create/content-edit-with-clara — Edit with Clara: type "Make the opening shorter.", Send, the draft text updates -->

**You should see:** Clara answers in the panel and the draft text changes.

If Clara is still writing, you see "Clara is still writing this draft. You can ask for changes once it's ready." If the draft came out of a conversation you can't open, you see "This draft came out of a conversation that isn't available to you". **Open conversation** appears when you can open it.

## Edit it yourself

1. Click **Edit draft** (**Edit** on a report).
2. Change the text under **Draft (Markdown)**. Newsletters also have **Subject line** and **Preview text**, and video posts have **Video title**.
3. Click **Save changes**. The status shows **Unsaved changes** until you do.

<!-- CLIP: create/content-edit-save — Edit draft: change a sentence in Draft (Markdown), Save changes -->

> **Note:** If someone changes the draft while you are editing, you see "This draft changed elsewhere. Your edits are preserved here. Copy them before loading the latest version." Click **Discard edits and reload** to load the latest version.

## Check the claims

A claim is a statement in a draft, such as a number or a fact about your product. Clara checks each one against your knowledge. The **Make it yours** panel says "A first draft, grounded in your context. Give it a read before sharing it with the world."

1. Read "Checked N claims against your knowledge base" and how many could not be confirmed.
2. Each flagged claim is marked **Not supported** or **Unconfirmed**. Check it against your sources, or edit the draft.
3. Add an optional note ("Add a note (optional)") and click **Acknowledge**.
4. If you edit the draft, click **Check again**. Once the text changes, "This version has changed since it was checked. Previous review results no longer apply."

If the claims haven't been checked yet, you see "This draft's claims haven't been checked. Review them yourself before publishing."

## Approve it

For content Clara can't publish itself, such as articles, release notes and emails, the page shows **Approve**. When claims need review it reads **Acknowledge and approve**.

1. Click **Approve**. You see "Approved."
2. The page shows "Approved by" followed by your name and the date.

> **Note:** **Approve** records that your team approved this draft. It does not publish or schedule anything. Approval is tied to the exact text, so editing after approval asks for approval again. Posts Clara can publish, such as a LinkedIn post, show **Publish now** and **Schedule post** instead. See [Review and share your work](/docs/publish/review-and-approvals).

## Download it

1. Click **Download**.
2. Choose **Markdown (.md)**, **Microsoft Word (.docx)** or **PDF (.pdf)**. When the draft has images, **With images (.zip)** is offered too.
3. The menu **More actions** has **Copy** and **Delete**.

Word and PDF only appear when the service can produce them.

## Share a private draft

**About this draft** shows **Visibility**. A draft made in a private chat reads "Only you" (or names the people the chat is shared with). Click **Share with organization**, or **Share with product members** when the draft has a product, to make it an ordinary draft. **Make private again** reverses it.

## If generation failed

A failed draft shows **Generation failed** with these choices:

- **Try again** runs it again.
- **Accept anyway** keeps the draft as it is. The points the review raised stay on it as claims to review.
- **Retry with notes** opens "What should be different this time?" Write notes and click **Retry with these notes**.
- **View details and retry** opens [the generation page](/docs/create/generation-retry).

## Other hand-offs

Depending on the format and your connections, the page can also show **Send with your email platform** (newsletters, through listmonk or Mailchimp), **Publish on WordPress** and **Send to Notion**. For a carousel, **Edit carousel** opens [the carousel studio](/docs/create/carousels). These belong to the publishing pages.

## FAQ

**What is a claim?** A statement in the draft, such as a number or a feature, that Clara checks against your knowledge. A claim it can't confirm is flagged for you to review.

**Why can't I approve?** Approving needs the right permission, and unsaved edits must be saved first. If the draft hasn't finished or failed, fix that first.

**Does approving publish?** No. **Approve** only records the approval.

**How do I share a private draft?** Use **Share with organization** under **Visibility** in **About this draft**, or share the chat it came from. See [Chat history and sharing](/docs/chat/chats).
