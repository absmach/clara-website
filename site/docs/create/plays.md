# Plays

A play is a ready-made job for one kind of work: a LinkedIn post, a blog post, release notes or a whole launch campaign. You pick the play, give Clara a short brief, and Clara writes from your brand guidelines, products and sources. Plays are how you hand off a one-off piece of work. For work that repeats, give a role to an agent instead. See [Build your marketing team](/docs/automate/agents).

**Where to find it:** sidebar → **Plays**

## Find a play

The page is titled "A little inspiration. A head start." and says "Choose a play. Bring your context. Make it your own."

- Type in **Find a play** ("Find a play…") to search by name or description.
- Use the tabs to narrow the list: **All plays**, **Content**, **Campaigns**, **Research** and **Studios**.
- If nothing matches, you see "No plays match" and "Try a different search or choose All plays."
- The banner "Something new on the horizon?" ("Give your next product launch a clear, connected story.") has **Plan a launch**.

Each card shows its action: **Start play**, **Open** or **Open studio**.

## Run a play

1. Open **Plays** and click a card, for example **LinkedIn post**.
2. The dialog says "A little context is all we need to get started." It lists "You'll get" the drafts you can expect.
3. Under **Product context**, choose a product, or leave **Organization-level**.
4. Write the brief. Its label depends on the play. For example: "Announce the new dashboard in Acme Analytics to marketing managers."
5. Optional: under **Include images**, choose **Follow brief**, **Include** or **Exclude**.
6. Optional: open **More options · Skills & guidance** to add skills for this run and use **Anything else to keep in mind?**
7. Click **Run now**.

**You should see:** a chat named after the play opens, and the draft appears in the **Draft preview** panel when it is ready. It is also listed in [My work](/docs/create/my-work).

The footer of the dialog says "Uses your product context". The **Brand guidelines** panel shows "Organization default · Applied automatically", and **View guidelines** opens them. See [Brand guidelines](/docs/context/brand).

> **Note:** Nothing is published by a play. Drafts go through approval. Running a content play needs the Editor role or an organization admin. Without it, the dialog says "You can't run this play. You have view-only access. Ask an organization admin for the Editor role to do this." Campaign plays need the same.

## Content plays

Each content play writes one draft: **Blog post**, **LinkedIn post**, **X post**, **X thread**, **Facebook Page post**, **Threads post**, **Bluesky post**, **Mastodon post**, **Instagram post**, **YouTube video post**, **TikTok video post**, **Instagram Reel**, **Threads video post**, **X video post**, **LinkedIn video post**, **Facebook video post**, **Facebook Reel**, **Release notes**, **Newsletter**, **Reddit comment** and **Carousel**.

The video-post plays also ask for **Video (optional)**: pick an approved video, or Clara starts one for you to approve. The **Carousel** play asks where it will run, the number of **Slides** (3 to 10), **Language** and an optional source article. See [Carousels](/docs/create/carousels).

## Campaign plays

A campaign play turns one brief into a set of related drafts: **Product Launch**, **Version Release**, **Conference Visit**, **Engineering Deep-Dive**, **Case Study**, **Thought Leadership**, **Industry report**, **Competitive Positioning**, **Problem Awareness** and **Roundup**. The dialog asks for an optional **Name**, the **Product context**, a **Campaign brief** and an optional **Launch date** with a **Timezone**.

With a launch date, Clara puts each piece on the Calendar on its day, waiting for your approval. Nothing is published until you approve it. See [Campaigns](/docs/create/campaigns).

## Research and studio plays

- **Competitive positioning analysis** asks for **Competitor websites** and, optionally, **Your website** (it defaults to your organization's website). Clara reads the sites and writes five documents, saved in My work: a company analysis, a strategy framework, positioning recommendations, a combined competitive analysis and a positioning brief. Earlier runs are listed under **Recent analyses** on the play's page.
- **Competitors** and **Media & Influencers** open their own pages (**Open studio**).
- **Improve website visibility** opens its own setup.
- **Video** opens the video studio, and **Custom workflow** opens the workflow builder. See [Build your marketing team](/docs/automate/agents).

## FAQ

**What is the difference between a play and a chat request?** A play gives Clara a fixed recipe and asks for the details it needs up front. In a chat you describe the work in your own words, and Clara picks the right play. See [Home and New chat](/docs/chat/new-chat).

**Which plays make several drafts?** The campaign plays. They plan a set of pieces from one brief. Every other content play makes one draft.

**Can I make my own plays?** Not as plays. To automate your own process, build a workflow. See [Build your marketing team](/docs/automate/agents).

**Why is a play greyed out?** You may have view-only access (the dialog says so), or, for **Video**, the video renderer may not be set up. The dialog shows the reason.
