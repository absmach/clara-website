# From a brief to a first draft

Plays are ready-made jobs for common formats. Pick one, give Clara a brief and review the draft it returns.

**Where to find it:** sidebar → **Plays**

## Choose a starting point

Open **Plays** to browse the available formats. Clara includes starting points for articles, social posts, threads, release notes and carousels. Choose the format for the reader and the channel, not just the length of the idea.

## Write the brief

Give Clara four things: the topic, the audience, the intended format and the takeaway. Point to the relevant product context and mention any constraints. A useful brief says what the reader should understand or do after reading.

> Create a short article for existing users explaining the new project overview. Use the release notes. Show one everyday example, include the current limitations, and finish with the first step to try it.

## Review the result in context

Read the draft alongside the material it draws on. Check names, feature availability, links and examples. Ask for a specific revision, such as shortening the opening or explaining a technical term, instead of asking for "better" copy.

## Build a connected campaign

For a launch or release, use a campaign play to organize related pieces. Keep the central facts consistent while adapting each piece to its channel. A social post can introduce the change, an article can explain it, and release notes can give users the precise details.

## Explore visual formats

Carousels turn an idea into a sequence of slides. Video studio works from a brief and supplied footage, images or audio, with preview and revision before export. See [Explore carousels and video](/#visuals).
