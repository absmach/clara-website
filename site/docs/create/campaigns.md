# Campaigns

A campaign is one story told through several drafts: a blog post, social posts, release notes and more, all written from the same brief. You start one from a campaign play. It is the way to hand off a launch or a release as a whole. For work that keeps repeating, use an agent. See [Build your marketing team](/docs/automate/agents).

**Where to find it:** sidebar → **Campaigns**

## Start a campaign

1. Open **Campaigns** and click **Start a campaign**. It opens [Plays](/docs/create/plays) on the **Campaigns** tab.
2. Choose a campaign play, for example **Product Launch**.
3. Optional: enter a **Name**.
4. Choose a **Product context**.
5. Write the **Campaign brief**: what you are promoting, and what the audience should understand or do.
6. Optional: set a **Launch date (optional)** and **Timezone**.
7. Click **Run now**.

With a launch date, Clara puts each piece on the Calendar on its day, waiting for your approval. Nothing is published until you approve it.

> **Note:** Campaign plays need the Editor role or an organization admin.

## The campaigns list

**Campaigns** says "One story. Every piece of work, together." Each campaign shows its product and playbook, its brief, a status badge, the launch time and progress such as "3 of 5 drafts ready" with the number that failed. The status is **Queued**, **In progress**, **Drafts ready**, **Completed with errors** or **Failed**. Click a campaign, or **Open campaign**, to open it.

With none, you see "No campaigns yet" ("Start one from a campaign play and Clara drafts every asset from a single brief.").

## The campaign page

The page shows "Drafts are generated, not published. Review each piece before sharing it with your audience." When you set a launch date, it also says how many pieces are on the Calendar around your launch.

Four numbers sum it up: **Drafts ready**, **Needing attention** (drafts that failed or hold claims Clara could not confirm), **Formats in this story** and **People with access**.

**Campaign work** lists every piece with **Title**, **Timing**, **Status** and **Actions**. A piece's badge says "Draft ready", "Draft ready, images failed", or "1 claim to review" (or "N claims to review"). **The campaign brief** below the table expands to show the original brief.

Open the menu "Actions for" a piece to see:

- **Open full draft**: review and edit the full document.
- **Copy Markdown**.
- **Download Markdown**, **Download Word** and **Download PDF**.
- **Retry generation** for a failed piece, which opens the [generation page](/docs/create/generation-retry).
- **Delete draft**: "Delete this draft? This can't be undone."

## Review the drafts

1. Open each piece with **Open full draft**.
2. Check its claims and edit it. See [The content page](/docs/create/content-page).
3. Approve it, or hand it to publishing. See [Review and share your work](/docs/publish/review-and-approvals).

## Add a draft

1. Click **Add a draft**. The dialog says "Choose what to add to" followed by the campaign name.
2. Under **Content**, choose a format, such as **Blog post** or **LinkedIn post**.
3. Under "What should it cover?", write what the piece should say and who it is for.
4. Click **Add draft**.

The campaign's brief, goal and audience are applied automatically.

## Share a campaign

1. Click **People & access**. The dialog is called "Campaign access".
2. Under **Person**, choose a member of your organization, for example Jane Doe.
3. Under **Role**, choose **Viewer** or **Manager**.
4. Click **Grant access**, then **Done**.

Viewers can read the campaign. Managers can also edit it and manage its people. Organization admins can always manage it. To take access away, click **Remove**. A group of campaigns can be shared at once. See [Campaign folders](/docs/create/campaign-folders).

> **Note:** Add a draft and People & access are for people who manage the campaign. If you open a campaign you can't access, you see "That campaign wasn't found, or you don't have access to it."

## Results

When reporting is set up, a **Campaign results** panel shows sessions, signups and activation events, with **Refresh** and **Pause reporting**. Open **Reporting settings** to connect it. This is optional.

## FAQ

**What is the difference between a campaign and a play?** A campaign is what a campaign play creates: a set of related drafts with their own page, progress and access. Other plays make one draft.

**Can I change the brief later?** The brief is saved with the campaign and shown under **The campaign brief**. To cover something new, use **Add a draft**.

**Where do campaign pieces go in the calendar?** With a launch date, each piece is placed around it and waits for your approval.

**How do I retry a failed piece?** Open its menu and choose **Retry generation**.
