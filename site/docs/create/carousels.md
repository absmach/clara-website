# Carousels

A carousel is one story told as a deck of slides. Clara makes a LinkedIn version, which is a PDF, and an Instagram version, which is a set of portrait JPEGs. Every slide uses your brand colours, fonts and logo.

**Where to find it:** sidebar → **Plays** → **Carousel**, then **Edit carousel** on the draft

## Make a carousel

1. Open **Plays** and click **Carousel**.
2. Under **Product context**, choose a product.
3. Write the brief, for example "Three things to check before choosing an analytics tool."
4. Under "Where will this run?", choose the channels. A channel shows **Connected** or **Not connected**. Clara can still make the slides without a connection.
5. Set the number of **Slides** (3 to 10, default 6), the **Language**, and optionally a **Source article (optional)**.
6. Click **Run now**.

When it is ready, open the draft and click **Edit carousel**.

## Edit the slides

The **Carousel studio** says "One story, in each channel's format." and "Shape your briefing, add real product visuals, and prepare it for sharing."

1. Choose a tab: **LinkedIn · PDF** or **Instagram · JPEGs**. On a small screen, switch between **Edit** and **Slides**.
2. Pick a slide in **Your slides**. Use **Move slide earlier** and **Move slide later** to reorder. The preview is portrait, 4:5.
3. Edit **Slide headline**, **Slide text** and **Source / credit**.
4. Under **Visual**, choose **Screenshot or image**, **Terminal output** or, where available, **Generated illustration**.
   - For **Terminal output**, paste the real command and output under **Paste real command and output**.
   - For an illustration, use **Generate illustration**, **Change it** or **Remove**.
   - For a screenshot, use **Add a product visual**: **Use as**, **Choose image** and **Attach to slide**. **Remove screenshot** undoes it.
5. Under **Brand and post caption**, set **Carousel title**, **Brand name**, **Accent color**, **Typography** (**Modern sans**, **Editorial serif** or **Technical mono**) and the **Post caption** for each channel.
6. Click **Save changes**.

## Approve and export

1. Under **Ready to share**, click **Approve current revision**. It says "Approve the saved revision, then schedule it in Calendar for each channel, or download it."
2. Click **Download PDF** for LinkedIn, or **Download images + caption** for Instagram.
3. For Instagram, **Make Instagram images** makes the 1080×1350 JPEGs so you can check them before publishing.

> **Note:** You can't approve with unsaved changes, or while illustrations are still being made ("Waiting for the illustrations to finish."). If one failed, you see "An illustration failed. Make it again or remove it." Downloads need the export service. When it isn't available, the download is greyed out with the reason.

Scheduling and publishing a carousel happen from the content page and the Calendar. See [Review and share your work](/docs/publish/review-and-approvals).

## FAQ

**How many slides can I have?** From 3 to 10 when you run the play. The studio lets you reorder slides but not add or delete them.

**Why is Approve disabled?** You have unsaved changes, illustrations are still being made or one failed, or the revision is already approved.

**Can I upload screenshots?** Yes. Use **Add a product visual**. Screenshots can be PNG, JPEG or WebP up to 10 MB, and logos can be PNG, JPEG or SVG up to 1 MB.
