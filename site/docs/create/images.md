# Images on a draft

Some drafts come with images, such as a cover image for a blog post or a picture for a social post. The **Post images** panel lets you check them, make them again, swap in your own or remove them.

**Where to find it:** sidebar → **My work** → a draft → **Post images**

> **Note:** The panel appears where image generation is turned on, or when the draft already has images. Brand guidelines supply your logo and colours. See [Brand guidelines](/docs/context/brand).

## What the panel shows

The panel says "Included with this draft when published or downloaded." Each image has a label:

- **Cover image**, **Inline image** or **Post image**
- **Thread post** followed by a number, or **Thread post image**
- **Slide image**

Each image also shows its alt text and where it came from: **Generated**, **Rendered by Clara** or **Uploaded**. While Clara works you see "Planning images", "Making images" or "Waiting to be made".

A post takes up to four images on LinkedIn, X, Bluesky and Mastodon, one on Instagram, and up to ten on Facebook, Threads, blog posts, X threads and carousels.

## Choose images when you run a play

In a play's dialog, **Include images** has three choices:

- **Follow brief**: images only when the brief asks for them.
- **Include**: Clara adds an image even if the brief doesn't ask for one.
- **Exclude**: no images, whatever the brief says.

## Make an image again

1. On the image, click **Regenerate**. For an image that failed, the button reads **Retry**.
2. In "Regenerate this image", leave the box empty to make it again from the same plan, or describe the change, for example "warmer colours, a closer view".
3. Click **Regenerate**.

## Use your own image

1. On the image, click **Replace**, and choose a PNG, JPEG or WebP file up to 20 MB.
2. To add one more image, click **Add image to post**.
3. Choose **Where it goes**, write the **Alt text** ("What the image shows, for someone who can't see it") and click **Upload**.

Other actions on each image are **Download** and **Remove** ("Remove this image from the draft?").

## If images fail

You see "Images failed" and "The text is ready, but its images aren't." Click **Retry images**, or **Continue without them**. The draft can still be approved and published with the images that are ready.

> **Note:** Managing images needs the Editor role or an organization admin. Images don't change the text of the draft.

## FAQ

**Why did my draft get no images?** Image generation may be off, the format may not take images, or you chose **Exclude** or left **Follow brief** with a brief that didn't ask for one.

**Can I use my own photo?** Yes. Use **Replace**, or **Add image to post**.

**Are the images used when I publish?** Yes. The panel says they are included when the draft is published or downloaded.
