This page describes this marketing website, not the future Clara service or a connected product account.

Interactive examples

The campaign examples use prewritten fictional content. Switching formats and copying samples happens in your browser. These interactions do not send prompts to an AI service or publish content.

Early-access inquiries

The form prepares an email in your email application. This website does not submit or store the form’s contents. You choose whether to send the email to info@absmach.eu. If you send it, Abstract Machines receives the information you include and can use it to respond to your inquiry.

Cookies and analytics

This website’s code does not set advertising or analytics cookies and does not include third-party analytics. The hosting service may process connection information and use access-related cookies, including when a private preview requires sign-in. Those hosting functions are separate from this website’s code.

External links

Links to Abstract Machines and Atomic open other websites with their own privacy information. Fonts and site assets are served with this website.

Contact

For questions about this website or information you have emailed to the team, contact info@absmach.eu.
