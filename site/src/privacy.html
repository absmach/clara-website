A clear view of what happens here.

This page describes this marketing website, not the future Clara service or a connected product account. Last updated 25 November 2026.

Who is responsible

This website is run by Abstract Machines, the team behind Clara. You can reach us at info@absmach.eu.

Interactive examples

The product tour and the example agent week use fictional content and run entirely in your browser. They do not send prompts to an AI service or publish content.

Early-access requests

When you request early access, we receive your name, email address, product or company, what you’d like help with, and any campaign tags in the page address (such as utm_source). We store them in our mailing system, Listmonk. We use them to review your request, reply about early access, and send emails related to early access. We send you one confirmation email when you sign up. Every later email includes an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing is respected even if you submit the form again. You can ask us to delete your details at any time.

Contact messages

When you use the contact form, we receive your name, email address, company if you give one, topic and message. They are emailed to the Abstract Machines team inbox so we can reply. They are not added to a mailing list.

Bot protection

Both forms use Cloudflare Turnstile to check that a person is sending them. Turnstile runs a short check in your browser and shares signals such as your IP address and browser details with Cloudflare for that purpose. The check loads only when you start filling in a form. See Cloudflare’s Turnstile privacy addendum.

Cookies and analytics

This website’s code does not set advertising or analytics cookies and does not include third-party analytics. The hosting service may process connection information and use access-related cookies, including when a private preview requires sign-in. Those hosting functions are separate from this website’s code.

External links

Links to Abstract Machines and Atomic open other websites with their own privacy information. Fonts and site assets are served with this website.

Your choices

To see, correct, or delete what you’ve sent us, or to stop receiving emails, contact info@absmach.eu or use the contact form with the Privacy topic.
