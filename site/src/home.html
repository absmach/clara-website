What is Clara?

Clara is an AI marketing team for founders and product teams. Start in chat or with content Plays to create articles, social posts, and campaigns in your brand voice. Workflows make the process repeatable; agents take on ongoing marketing roles, with the level of autonomy you choose.

Who is it for?

Founders, product marketers, and small teams who need to explain what they’re building. Clara is especially useful when your story lives across product docs, technical material, and release notes.

Can I review content before it goes out?

Yes. Assistant and Co-pilot ask before external or irreversible actions such as publishing. Autopilot can publish automatically, and you can keep approval requirements for specific actions, channels, products, or agents. Publishing still needs the right connections and permissions.

Does Clara need to know my product first?

Useful context makes better drafts. Add product documentation, repositories, website sources, or files, then define your brand voice and audience. Clara uses that context in its work; you should still check the finished content.

Are these real product screenshots?

The product gallery shows real screenshots captured from the running Clara application in a demo workspace. The content examples and hero artwork are fictional Fieldnotes illustrations; they do not generate or publish anything.

How can I try Clara? What does it cost?

We’re preparing Clara for early access. Tell us about your product and we’ll discuss availability. Pricing and launch dates are not announced here.
