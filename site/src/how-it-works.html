01 Bring your world

The best starting point? What you already know.

Add the sources that explain your product: docs, GitHub repositories, websites, or files. Clara builds a knowledge base and draws on it when drafting.

  • Product context. Each product keeps its own knowledge, so a new brief starts from the right material.
  • A recognizable voice. Define audience, voice, and writing rules, with product-specific guidance where needed.
  • Your own methods. Reusable skills capture how your team likes things done.

02 Start the conversation

Say what you’re trying to say.

Start in chat or pick a Play. Explain what changed and who it matters to. Clara drafts from your brief and the relevant product context.

“Turn this release into a LinkedIn post and an article for product teams. Keep it practical.”
  • One idea, one piece. A post, article, X thread, or release announcement for a specific audience and channel.
  • A connected campaign. Use a launch or release playbook to keep related pieces around the same story.

03 Give it another dimension

Some ideas need to be seen.

Go beyond a text draft with Clara’s visual tools.

  • Carousels. LinkedIn PDFs and Instagram image sets, with screenshots and terminal visuals, unfolding slide by slide.
  • Video studio. Start from a brief and your own footage, images, or audio. Preview, ask for revisions, then export an MP4.

Video needs a configured rendering service and AI provider.

04 Connect the steps

Your process. Built on a canvas.

In Workflows, connect blocks for research, writing, decisions, review, and publishing. Or describe the sequence and let Build with Clara set it up.

  • Branch and bring work together. Run an article and social posts in parallel, add conditions, or loop over a list.
  • Choose the trigger. On request, on a schedule, from a Clara event, or by webhook.
  • See what happened. Follow each run in Activity. Pause an agent or a single job whenever plans change.

A trigger starts the work. Approvals, connected accounts, and your schedule decide when anything goes live.

05 Meet your team

Agents. Marketing that keeps going.

Agents are team members with ongoing jobs. Give one a goal, your product context, and permission to act. It works on schedule and responds to events, without waiting for your next prompt.

  • ✎

    Content Writer

    Researches topics and drafts articles from your product knowledge.

  • ↗

    Social Manager

    Plans your week, fills calendar gaps, and adapts posts for other channels.

  • ◎

    Search Visibility

    Checks your website, finds content gaps, and drafts improvements.

  • ⌕

    Competitor Analyst

    Tracks competitor updates and brings the highlights into a weekly digest.

06 Planner & Calendar

Plan the work. See it on Calendar.

The planner weighs your priorities, product context, market signals, and what your agents have learned. It proposes objectives and books jobs for your agents on Calendar.

  • Set your priorities. Tell Clara what matters now in Calendar’s Plan view.
  • Review the week. Posts and agent jobs share one calendar. Edit a brief, move a booking, or reject work with a reason.
  • Plan again. Clara plans weekly or on request, and keeps your bookings and edits.

Bookings show when work starts. Approvals, autonomy settings, and the run budget still apply.

07 Choose how much to delegate

Your marketing team. Ready for Autopilot.

In Settings → Autonomy, choose what Clara may do on its own. Your guardrails and permissions always apply.

  1. Assistant

    Clara suggests work. Nothing runs on its own.

  2. Co-pilot

    Internal work runs automatically. Publishing and other external actions ask first.

  3. Autopilot

    Work runs automatically, publishing included. Keep approval for any action, channel, or product.

Set posting limits, quiet hours, and topics to avoid. Pause Autopilot whenever you need to.
